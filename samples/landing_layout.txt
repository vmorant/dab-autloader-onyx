The manifest (samples/manifest_*.json) carries load_spec: target table, source_path,
load_type, keys, sequence... It is the table's only configuration.

Rules
- One run_id=... folder per extraction; files are never overwritten.
- The manifest is written once all the run's parquet files are written.
- run_id = YYYYMMDDTHHMMSSZ (UTC of the extraction) + "_" + CLEX run id.
- Manifest data_path = data/{load_spec.source_path}/run_id={run_id}/
- CDC: operation column (I/U/D) and sequence increasing per PK, never NULL.
- Once a table is registered, CLEX cannot change its source_path, load_type,
  keys, sequence_by or operation_column (SPEC_CONFLICT). Everything else, by bumping spec_version.
- The landing is purged by lifecycle (30 days in pro); bronze is the permanent record.
